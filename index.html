2

3
Press again
Your words are typed at the cursor in whatever app is in focus.
Privacy First

Your voice never leaves your machine.

KeySpeak uses Whisper, an open-source speech recognition model that runs entirely on-device. There is no cloud backend, no account, and no subscription.

The only permissions requested are Microphone (to capture audio) and Accessibility (to type text into other apps). Neither is used for anything else.
